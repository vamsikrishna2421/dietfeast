Prep Schedule & Process

⏰ Daily Prep Routine (2-3 hours total)

For 5-10 meals, estimate 2-3 hours per day

1

Morning (6-8 AM) - Protein Prep (45 min)

  • Chicken: Thaw, slice, season, pan-sear 5 min each side
  • Shrimp: Thaw in cold water, peel, pat dry, quick sear 2 min each side
  • Eggs: Scramble in large batch, cool
  • Tofu: Cube, pan-fry until golden
2

Mid-Morning (8-9 AM) - Vegetable Prep (30 min)

  • Chop: Broccoli, peppers, mushrooms, spinach, carrots, cabbage
  • Pre-cook (optional): Some vegetables to improve texture
  • Pre-measure: Portion vegetables into containers
3

Afternoon (12-2 PM) - Meal Assembly (45 min for 10 meals)

  • Rinse shirataki, dry-pan 3 minutes
  • Add vegetables (already prepped)
  • Add cooked protein (already prepared)
  • Add sauces & seasonings
  • Pack in container, label with date & recipe name
  • Cool to room temp, refrigerate

🧊 Food Safety & Shelf Life

  • Refrigeration Required: Keep below 40°F at all times
  • Shelf Life: 5-7 days in refrigerator
  • Labeling: Date & recipe name on every meal
  • Reheating: Microwave 2-3 minutes
