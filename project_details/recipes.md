# DIET FEAST - Current Recipe Collection

## Overview
All recipes use shirataki noodles as the base and are designed to feel filling while staying low calorie. Current approved protein options are chicken, shrimp, eggs, tofu, and paneer. Mushrooms stay on the vegetable side of the menu.

Every recipe starts with the same noodle prep:

1. Rinse 1 pack (7oz) shirataki noodles under cold water for 2 minutes.
2. Drain well and pat dry.
3. Add noodles to a hot, dry pan over medium-high heat.
4. Stir for 3-4 minutes until chewy and slightly translucent.
5. Transfer to the meal container or return to the pan during final assembly.

## Recipe 1: Grilled Chicken Soy-Ginger

### Nutritional Info
- **Calories:** 145
- **Protein:** 24g
- **Shelf Life:** 5-6 days

### Ingredients
- Shirataki noodles
- 3oz cooked boneless chicken leg meat
- 1 cup broccoli
- 1/2 cup mushrooms
- 1.5 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- Ginger, garlic, black pepper

### Cooking Steps
1. Prepare shirataki using the standard noodle prep.
2. Saute mushrooms, broccoli, ginger, and garlic.
3. Add cooked chicken, soy sauce, and sesame oil.
4. Toss with noodles and heat through.
5. Cool, seal, label, and refrigerate.

## Recipe 2: Shrimp Lime-Cilantro

### Nutritional Info
- **Calories:** 160
- **Protein:** 22g
- **Shelf Life:** 4-5 days

### Ingredients
- Shirataki noodles
- 4oz cooked shrimp
- Bell peppers
- Carrots
- Lime juice
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Red pepper flakes
- Cilantro

### Cooking Steps
1. Prepare shirataki using the standard noodle prep.
2. Whisk lime juice, soy sauce, sesame oil, and red pepper flakes.
3. Saute bell peppers and carrots until just tender.
4. Add shrimp and sauce, then warm gently.
5. Layer over noodles and garnish with cilantro.

## Recipe 3: Egg Fried Noodles

### Nutritional Info
- **Calories:** 140
- **Protein:** 18g
- **Shelf Life:** 3-4 days

### Ingredients
- Shirataki noodles
- 2 eggs
- Mixed vegetables
- Onion
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Ginger and garlic

### Cooking Steps
1. Prepare shirataki using the standard noodle prep.
2. Scramble eggs in a hot pan and set aside.

## Recipe 4: Tofu Miso

### Nutritional Info
- **Calories:** 150
- **Protein:** 18g
- **Shelf Life:** 5-6 days

### Ingredients
- Shirataki noodles
- 4oz firm tofu
- Spinach or bok choy
- Mushrooms
- Miso paste
- Low-sodium broth or water
- Sesame oil
- Garlic and ginger

### Cooking Steps
1. Prepare shirataki using the standard noodle prep.
2. Pan-sear tofu cubes until lightly golden.
3. Saute mushrooms, garlic, ginger, and greens.
4. Dissolve miso into warm broth.
5. Combine tofu, vegetables, noodles, and broth.

## Recipe 11: Paneer Tikka Noodles

### Nutritional Info
- **Calories:** 165
- **Protein:** 14g
- **Shelf Life:** 4-5 days

### Ingredients
- Shirataki noodles
- Paneer cubes
- Bell peppers
- Onion
- Plain Greek yogurt
- Garam masala
- Turmeric
- Cumin
- Garlic and ginger

### Cooking Steps
1. Prepare shirataki using the standard noodle prep.
2. Mix yogurt, garam masala, turmeric, cumin, garlic, and ginger.
3. Lightly sear paneer cubes so the edges brown.
4. Saute bell peppers and onion.
5. Add paneer, tikka sauce, and noodles, then toss gently.

### Prep Note
Paneer is calorie-dense compared with tofu, so keep the paneer portion controlled and use vegetables for volume.

## Recipe 12: Mushroom Garlic Pepper

### Nutritional Info
- **Calories:** 120
- **Protein:** 10g
- **Shelf Life:** 4-5 days

### Ingredients
- Shirataki noodles
- Mixed mushrooms
- Spinach
- Cabbage
- Onion
- Garlic
- Black pepper
- Light soy sauce
- Optional: extra tofu or egg for customers who want more protein

### Cooking Steps
1. Prepare shirataki using the standard noodle prep.
2. Saute mushrooms until they release water and begin to brown.
3. Add onion and cabbage, then cook until softened.
4. Add spinach, garlic, black pepper, and light soy sauce.
5. Toss with noodles and portion.

### Prep Note
Mushrooms shrink while cooking. Start with more raw mushrooms than the final portion size suggests.

## Storage and Reheating

- Store all meals below 40°F.
- Cool meals fully before sealing to prevent condensation.
- Label every container with recipe name and prep date.
- Microwave 2-3 minutes, stirring halfway.
- Broth meals should be reheated with the lid slightly ajar.
- Shrimp and egg meals are best earlier in the week.
