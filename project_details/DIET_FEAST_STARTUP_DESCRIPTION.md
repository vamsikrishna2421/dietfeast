# DIET FEAST - Startup Description

## 🎯 Executive Summary

**Diet Feast** is an ultra-low-calorie meal prep delivery service operating in the Worcester, MA area. We deliver fresh, ready-to-eat meals that are 100-200 calories but feel like 500+ calorie portions, at just $9.99 per meal—40% cheaper than competitors like HelloFresh ($13.99-16.99) and Factor.

Using shirataki noodles (10 cal/pack) as our base, we create massive, satisfying volumes that make dieters feel full while staying in their calorie targets. Perfect for fitness enthusiasts, weight loss seekers, and halal-conscious consumers (no beef/pork).

**Launch:** 1-2 meals/day locally  
**Year 1 Target:** 10 meals/day = $1,285/month profit  
**Year 1 Profit:** $8,473+

## 🏭 Operations & Logistics

### Daily Prep Routine (2-3 hours)

**Morning (6-8 AM) - Protein Prep: 45 min**
- Thaw and cook chicken (pan-sear 5 min each side)
- Cook shrimp (2 min each side)
- Scramble eggs in batch with seasonings
- Pan-fry tofu cubes until golden

**Mid-Morning (8-9 AM) - Vegetable Prep: 30 min**
- Chop: broccoli, peppers, mushrooms, carrots, spinach, cabbage
- Pre-measure into portions
- Optional: pre-cook some vegetables for better texture

**Afternoon (12-2 PM) - Meal Assembly: 45 min for 10 meals**
1. Rinse shirataki noodles
2. Dry-pan 3 minutes (removes rubbery texture)
3. Add pre-cooked vegetables
4. Add protein portion
5. Add sauces & seasonings
6. Pack in microwave container
7. Label with date & recipe name
8. Cool to room temperature
9. Refrigerate

### Weekly Strategy

| Day | Activity | Time | Output |
|-----|----------|------|--------|
| Monday AM | Weekly shopping (Costco + local market) | 1-2 hrs | All ingredients |
| Tuesday | Batch cook: chicken, eggs | 2 hrs | 30-40 meals prepped |
| Wed-Fri | Daily assembly (3-5 meals/day) | 1.5 hrs/day | Fresh meals daily |
| Friday PM | Batch cook: shrimp, tofu, vegetables | 2 hrs | Weekend prep |
| Sat-Sun | Daily assembly (2-3 meals/day, on-demand) | 1 hr/day | Weekend meals |

### Shelf Life & Food Safety
- **Refrigeration required:** Keep below 40°F
- **Shelf life:** 5-7 days
- **Reheating:** Microwave 2-3 minutes
- **Labeling:** Date & recipe name on all meals
